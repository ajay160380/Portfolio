WIS 2026 Interactive Player

A full-screen video player driven by a Taiko no Tatsujin drum controller. A hit on the left or right side plays that side’s film once, then the loop resumes.

Client
KT
Where
KT booth, World IT Show 2026, COEX, Seoul
When
April 2026
Our part
Software development and every interaction

For KT’s booth at World IT Show 2026 we built a full-screen video player for Windows. With no input it repeats the loop video. A hit on the left of the drum plays film one, a hit on the right plays film two, and the player then returns to the loop. During playback a second hit on the same side restarts the film, and the other side is ignored. The drum registers as a USB gamepad, and its left and right buttons are assigned in the F12 settings screen by hitting the drum.

Key engineering

  • Full-screen Windows player on WebView, served by a local video server that handles HTTP Range requests
  • Idle/playing state machine over three preloaded videos (restart on a same-side hit, other side ignored, back to the loop at the end)
  • Learn-by-hit button mapping over unified keyboard, mouse and gamepad input (Gamepad API polling, rising-edge detection)
  • Single-executable delivery and an F12 operator panel for swapping video files, with settings saved to config.json
