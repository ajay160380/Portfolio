Me in a Genre

A Millie’s Library kiosk that finds the genre that suits a visitor from a face photo and a reading-style test, then makes a book cover with the visitor as its hero.

Client
Millie’s Library × KT Onmaji
Where
KT Onmaji flagship store, Gwanghwamun, Seoul
When
2026
Our part
Kiosk software

A kiosk for Millie’s Library and KT Onmaji. Visitors press start and have their face photographed, then take a reading-style test of ten questions about their days and feelings. From the photo and the test the kiosk makes a book cover with the visitor as its hero, with a note on the genre, and the image is saved from a QR code.

Key engineering

  • Portrait touch-kiosk interface: start, face photo and a ten-question reading-style test
  • A made-to-measure book cover and a note on the genre, from the face photo and the test result
  • The result image, saved from a QR code

System

  • Touch screen
  • Kiosk camera
  • Kiosk app
  • Book-cover generation
  • Your book cover
  • QR download
