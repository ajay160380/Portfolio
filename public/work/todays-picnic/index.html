Today’s Picnic

A kiosk whose travel-style test finds a taste for travel visitors didn’t know they had. It shows the recommended place in a film, then a travel guide and places to eat.

Client
KT Onmaji
Where
KT Onmaji flagship store, Gwanghwamun, Seoul
When
2026
Our part
Kiosk software

Today’s Picnic, a kiosk for KT Onmaji. Visitors read the guide on a picnic-themed start screen and answer questions about travel, such as who they would like to go with. While they wait, picnic illustrations and KT offers play; the recommended place appears first in a film, then on a result page with a travel guide. The last screen pairs the guide with places to eat.

Key engineering

  • Portrait touch-kiosk interface: start screen, guide and travel-style questions
  • A place in Korea recommended from the answers, introduced by a result film
  • KT offers shown while the result is on its way
  • Travel guide and places to eat, with a combined guide where no partner restaurant is listed

System

  • Touch screen
  • Kiosk app
  • Travel-style scoring
