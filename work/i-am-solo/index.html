I Am Solo in Gwanghwamun

A portrait kiosk for the pop-up of ENA’s I Am Solo. Visitors have their face photographed, get an AI character profile and a talent-show result, and take them home by QR code and as a print.

Client
KT Onmaji × ENA
Where
Pop-up at the KT Onmaji flagship store, Gwanghwamun, Seoul
When
2025
Our part
Kiosk software

A pop-up kiosk that makes visitors the star of ENA’s I Am Solo. They tap the start screen, choose a gender and have their face photographed, and an AI-made character profile appears. A talent-show result follows in the same visit, and the result can be saved from a QR code on screen or printed on site.

Prints and characters

  • A profile printed on site
  • Character profiles
  • Talent-show results

Key engineering

  • Portrait touch-kiosk interface from the start screen through gender choice and face photo to the profile
  • AI character profile and talent-show result made from the face photo
  • Results saved from a QR code on screen, and profiles printed on site
